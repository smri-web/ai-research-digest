## AI models & research

### An Evaluation Framework for Structured Audio Captions Validated by Controlled Perturbations
*Liang-Yuan Wu, Sripathi Sridhar, Mark Cartwright et al. · arXiv* · [https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21424v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21424v1)

- **What they studied:** The paper studied how to evaluate structured audio captions, which separate acoustic and semantic details.
- **Why they studied it:** Current evaluation methods for audio captions do not work well for these new structured formats.
- **Method:** They developed a multi-axis evaluation framework using LLM judges for semantics and computational metrics for acoustics. They validated it by introducing controlled errors into groundtruth data.
- **Findings and results:** The framework successfully identified real errors while ignoring minor wording changes.
- **Benefits and impact:** This framework provides a reliable way to assess the quality of structured audio descriptions.

## AI system design & engineering

### OpenForgeRL: Train Harness-native Agents in Any Environment
*Xiao Yu, Baolin Peng, Ruize Xu et al. · arXiv* · [https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21557v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21557v1)

- **What they studied:** AI agents use complex inference harnesses for reasoning and tool use. These harnesses include systems like Claude Code and OpenClaw.
- **Why they studied it:** Training these harness-based agents end-to-end is difficult with current open infrastructure. Existing training stacks cannot handle the stateful, multi-process nature of harness inference.
- **Method:** They developed OpenForgeRL, an open-source framework. It uses a lightweight proxy to record harness model calls as training data and a Kubernetes orchestrator for scalable rollouts.
- **Findings and results:** OpenForgeClaw achieved 31.7 pass^3 and 55.9 pass@3 on ClawEval, and 33.7 on QwenClawBench. OpenForgeGUI reached 37.7 on OSWorld-Verified, 63.0 on Online-Mind2Web, and 72.3 on WebVoyager. These results often surpassed open baselines and sometimes matched larger models.
- **Benefits and impact:** OpenForgeRL allows researchers to train and study agents directly in their deployment harnesses and environments. This decoupling of training and inference simplifies agent development and improvement.

### Toward Continuous Assurance for the Democratization of AI Agent Creation in Industry
*Natan Levy, Harel Berger · arXiv* · [https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21495v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21495v1)

- **What they studied:** The paper studied the reliability challenges of AI agents created by non-engineers in organizations.
- **Why they studied it:** AI agents built with low-code or no-code tools can silently degrade over time due to changing dependencies, creating a reliability gap.
- **Method:** They proposed a continuous assurance framework combining dependency mapping, readiness contracts, scheduled checks, diagnostics, and lifecycle governance.
- **Findings and results:** The framework assesses if an agent remains operationally ready. An initial prototype auditor and scenario-based assessment demonstrated practical checks and remediation guidance.
- **Benefits and impact:** This framework helps ensure the ongoing reliability of AI agents created by citizen developers.
