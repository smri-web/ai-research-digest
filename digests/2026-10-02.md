## AI models & research

### Where-OPD: Spatially Guided On-Policy Self-Distillation of MLLMs with Synthetic Scenes
*Sophia Sirko-Galouchenko, Monika Wysoczanska, Andrei Bursuc et al. · arXiv* · [https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02117v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02117v1)

- **What they studied:** Researchers studied how to improve multimodal large language models (MLLMs) using a method called on-policy self-distillation.
- **Why they studied it:** Applying self-distillation to MLLMs was largely unexplored, and existing methods for visual information had limitations.
- **Method:** They developed a self-distillation method where a teacher MLLM receives textual, spatially grounded guidance about relevant visual elements in synthetic scenes.
- **Findings and results:** This method consistently improved performance on counting, document, and chart understanding benchmarks, with a 3.23-point average gain on real-world perception benchmarks.
- **Benefits and impact:** The approach enables scalable, annotation-free post-training and shows that improvements from synthetic data transfer to real-world tasks.

### Mimir: Physics-Grounded LLM Agents for Long-Horizon Irrigation Control
*Yimeng Liu, Mi Zhang, Younsuk Dong et al. · arXiv* · [https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02038v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02038v1)

- **What they studied:** Researchers studied how large language model (LLM) agents perform in long-term physical control tasks, specifically irrigation.
- **Why they studied it:** Most LLM agent research focuses on short tasks, but physical control involves compounding errors and learning from experience without changing physical rules.
- **Method:** They developed Mimir, an LLM agent with two repair mechanisms: fast, numerical checking and revision of LLM outputs, and slow consolidation of failure patterns into persistent principles.
- **Findings and results:** Mimir achieved the lowest control cost among references and used 51% less irrigation than historical schedules. Removing forward simulation, verified revision, or persistent context increased control costs.
- **Benefits and impact:** This work shows how LLM agents can combine reasoning with self-improvement in physical systems while maintaining safety through numerical mechanisms.
