## AI models & research

### Certified Execution Harness for Agentic AI: Safe and Least-Intrusive Petri-Net Supervisory Control Between Reasoning and Action
*Chao Gu, Quanyan Zhu · arXiv* · [https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31376v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31376v1)

- **What they studied:** This paper studied safe tool execution for large language model (LLM) agents.
- **Why they studied it:** They addressed the problem of prompt injection attacks on LLM agents.
- **Method:** They used Petri nets to formalize the execution harness between an LLM and its tools as a supervisory control problem.
- **Findings and results:** They found that a conventional supervisor can be more intrusive than a stricter safe one in a replanning loop. A maximally permissive least-intrusive supervisor is a value-threshold refinement of the conventional one. Safe deployment for an autonomous release agent requires a supervisor-refusable step after rollout starts.
- **Benefits and impact:** This approach refuses injected requests if preconditions are unmet or budgets are spent, ensuring safety regardless of wording.

### How Ground Truth Shapes Retrieval Scores: Evidence-Aware Evaluation of a Retrieval-Augmented Generation System for Breast Lesions
*Miguel Mochizuki Silva, Samuel Ismael, Mateus C. Dias et al. · arXiv* · [https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31606v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31606v1)

- **What they studied:** Researchers studied how well a system finds relevant information for breast lesion management questions.
- **Why they studied it:** They wanted to improve how retrieval systems are evaluated, especially for medical information.
- **Method:** They used 18 documents and 18 questions, testing different chunk sizes and retriever settings. They measured retrieval using standard metrics like Recall@K and R-Precision.
- **Findings and results:** A hybrid retriever with cross-encoder reranking performed best, finding evidence in the top three results for 17 of 18 queries. Expanding relevance annotations increased recognized evidence without changing retrieval rankings.
- **Benefits and impact:** The study shows the importance of detailed relevance annotations for evaluating medical retrieval systems.

## AI & psychology / mental health

### Scaling Therapy Supervision: Assessing Psychotherapy Fidelity Using Large Language Models
*Arvind Pillai, Alexis Roach, Jason Holden et al. · arXiv* · [https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28772v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28772v1)

- **What they studied:** This paper studied how to automate the assessment of psychotherapy fidelity using large language models (LLMs). Fidelity assessment measures how well a therapist follows a treatment plan.
- **Why they studied it:** Manual expert review of therapy sessions is slow and labor-intensive. This limits the ability to scale therapy supervision and therapist training.
- **Method:** The researchers used LLMs to automate fidelity assessment and generate feedback from clinical transcripts. They tested three open-weight models with different task decomposition and reasoning strategies.
- **Findings and results:** Evidence-first reasoning improved fidelity estimation. The framework distinguished Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) from non-CBT sessions in an independent study. Mental health experts found the generated feedback actionable.
- **Benefits and impact:** This work offers a scalable solution for therapy supervision. It can help bridge the community mental health supervision gap.
