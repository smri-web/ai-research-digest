## AI models & research

### GraFT: A Training-Free Framework for Spatial Reasoning in Multimodal Large Language Models via 3D Scene Graphs
*Junqing Du, Fernando Ropero, Erkin Turkoz et al. · arXiv* · [https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03892v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03892v1)

- **What they studied:** Multimodal large language models (MLLMs) struggle with 3D spatial reasoning.
- **Why they studied it:** Current MLLMs are unreliable at precise geometric measurement, viewpoint transformations, and grounding fine-grained appearance.
- **Method:** They introduced GraFT, a training-free framework that provides 3D structure using a 3D scene graph (3DSG).
- **Findings and results:** GraFT improved all metrics on ScanQA, raising CIDEr by 27%. It improved frozen MLLMs on VSI-Bench by up to 65%.
- **Benefits and impact:** GraFT surpasses proprietary and open-source baselines, including some fine-tuned spatial models, without costly supervision or specific backbone coupling.

## AI system design & engineering

### Para-Pipe: Exploiting Hierarchical Operator Parallelism of ML Computational Graphs on SoCs
*Yujie Zhang, Huiying Lan, Ehsan Aghapour et al. · arXiv* · [https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04168v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04168v1)

- **What they studied:** This paper studied how to optimize deep learning performance on System-on-Chips (SoCs). It focused on balancing throughput and latency for complex neural networks.
- **Why they studied it:** Edge AI applications need better performance on SoCs. Current methods struggle with latency for complex neural networks.
- **Method:** They developed Para-Pipe, a framework that combines operator parallelism within and across pipeline stages. This framework fine-tunes parallelism levels to manage throughput and latency.
- **Findings and results:** Para-Pipe created multiple configurations that balanced throughput and latency. On an Amlogic SoC, it improved energy efficiency by 11.0% over pipelining and 23.3% over non-pipelined parallel execution.
- **Benefits and impact:** This approach reduces communication overhead and improves energy efficiency. It offers better performance for deep learning on edge devices.

## AI & human behavior

### CompanionSim: Synthetic Data for Evaluating Anthropomorphism in Human-AI Relationships
*Jacy Reese Anthis, Mark Díaz, Renee Shelby · arXiv* · [https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00250v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00250v1)

- **What they studied:** Researchers studied how people perceive AI chatbots that act like social companions.
- **Why they studied it:** People increasingly view AI as companions, but data on human-AI social interaction is scarce.
- **Method:** They created CompanionSim, a simulation framework with 2,240 simulated conversations, and tested it with human participants.
- **Findings and results:** Companionship behaviors in chatbots reduced likability, humanlikeness, and trust, especially among women and older participants.
- **Benefits and impact:** This work provides synthetic data and a framework for evaluating AI companionship behaviors.
