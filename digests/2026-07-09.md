## AI models & research

### UP: Unbounded Positive Asymmetric Optimization for Breaking the Exploration-Stability Dilemma
*Chongyu Fan, Pengfei Liu, Jingjia Huang et al. · arXiv* · [https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06987v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06987v1)

- **What they studied:** Researchers studied how to improve reinforcement learning (RL) for large language models (LLMs). They focused on the trade-off between exploring new options and maintaining stable training.
- **Key finding:** Current methods limit exploration by prematurely cutting off updates for potentially good but uncertain reasoning paths. A new method called UP allows more exploration while keeping training stable.
- **Why it matters:** This new method, UP, can improve how LLMs learn complex reasoning. It can be easily added to existing RL training systems.

### MedPMC: A Systematic Framework for Scaling High-Fidelity Medical Multimodal Data for Foundation Models
*Hyunjae Kim, Dain Kim, Pan Xiao et al. · arXiv* · [https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07673v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07673v1)

- **What they studied:** Researchers developed MedPMC, a system to create high-quality medical image-text data from scientific literature.
- **Key finding:** MedPMC produced 11 million image-text pairs from 6.1 million articles, significantly improving medical AI model performance.
- **Why it matters:** This new dataset and framework can accelerate the development of better multimodal AI for healthcare, even with fewer training examples.

### Native-speed vLLM transformers modeling backend
*Hugging Face · Hugging Face* · [https://huggingface.co/blog/native-speed-vllm-transformers-backend](https://huggingface.co/blog/native-speed-vllm-transformers-backend)

- **What it covers:** Hugging Face released a new vLLM backend for its transformers library. This integration allows vLLM to power inference for Hugging Face models.
- **Key takeaway:** The vLLM backend significantly speeds up large language model (LLM) inference within the Hugging Face ecosystem.
- **Why it matters:** Developers can now achieve native vLLM performance directly through the familiar Hugging Face transformers API, simplifying deployment and improving efficiency.

## AI system design & engineering

### The Key to Going Linear: Analysis-Driven Transformer Linearization
*Anna Kuzina, Paul N. Whatmough, Babak Ehteshami Bejnordi · arXiv* · [https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07706v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07706v1)

- **What they studied:** This paper looked at how to make transformer models process longer text more efficiently. They focused on changing how the model updates its internal state without retraining the main parts.
- **Key finding:** The researchers found that the softmax function in transformers uses specific mathematical projections. This explains why certain network designs work better for linearization.
- **Why it matters:** Their method makes large language models like LLaMA and Qwen, up to 32 billion parameters, handle long text much faster. It performs better than previous methods and matches complex systems for long-context tasks.
