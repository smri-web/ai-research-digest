## AI models & research

### Signals, Evidence, and Context: User Scrutiny in Real-World LLM Conversations
*Ahana Biswas, Yu-Ru Lin · arXiv* · [https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08232v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08232v1)

- **What they studied:** This paper studied how users interact with large language model (LLM) answers.
- **Why they studied it:** LLMs often provide answers without easily inspectable supporting evidence.
- **Method:** They analyzed 489,283 assistant-user conversations from ShareChat and WildChat.
- **Findings and results:** Users accept, check, or challenge LLM responses based on various cues. Explicit URLs are rare, appearing in only 1.33% of responses, while broad attribution is in 31.9%.
- **Benefits and impact:** Improving user verification requires better inspectable evidence, understanding user questions, and considering conversation history.

### Agent-R$^2$: Scaling Agents for Realistic Productivity Work via Real-Source Data Synthesis
*Teng Pan, Zixuan Wang, Yuchen Yan et al. · arXiv* · [https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08219v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08219v1)

- **What they studied:** Researchers studied how to improve large language models (LLMs) for realistic productivity tasks.
- **Why they studied it:** Current LLM agents struggle with complex, long-term tasks that require understanding diverse files and meeting specific requirements.
- **Method:** They developed Agent-R^2, a framework that synthesizes training data from real occupational examples and web-sourced files.
- **Findings and results:** Agent-R^2-4B, trained on 4,000 instances, outperformed Qwen3-235B-A22B-Thinking-2507 on two benchmarks, GDPval (51.0% vs. 48.0%) and OfficeQA (14.8% vs. 13.8%).
- **Benefits and impact:** This approach improves agent performance on productivity tasks and reduces superficial output.

### ProQuant: Progressive Quantization-aware Training for Edge MLLMs
*Yufei Xue, Yushi Huang, Jiawei Shao et al. · arXiv* · [https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08216v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08216v1)

- **What they studied:** Researchers studied how to make large multi-modal language models (MLLMs) work on devices with limited resources.
- **Why they studied it:** MLLMs need a lot of computing power and memory, which stops them from being used on edge devices.
- **Method:** They developed ProQuant, a training method that uses low-bit quantization for MLLMs. This method includes a self-relabeling scheme and a progressive block activation mechanism.
- **Findings and results:** ProQuant significantly improved performance over existing methods for models with 2B-8B parameters. For example, a W4A8 Qwen3-VL-2B-Instruct model showed a 6.6% average accuracy increase and only about a 1% performance drop compared to full precision.
- **Benefits and impact:** This work makes MLLMs more practical for real-world use on resource-constrained edge devices.

## AI & human behavior

### Securing the agentic enterprise with data lineage
*MIT Technology Review Insights · MIT Technology Review* · [https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/11/04/1147596/securing-the-agentic-enterprise-with-data-lineage/](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/11/04/1147596/securing-the-agentic-enterprise-with-data-lineage/)

- **What it covers:** AI agents can access and combine sensitive data from various internal systems without human oversight. This includes restricted pricing, internal communications, and archived quotes.
- **Why it matters now:** The lack of human checks in AI agent handoffs creates a risk of inaccurate or sensitive data being used in business processes.
- **Key takeaway:** Enterprises need to secure agentic AI systems, especially when they handle sensitive customer data.
- **Benefits and impact:** Implementing data lineage can help track data sources and ensure accuracy when AI agents are used for tasks like generating customer proposals.
